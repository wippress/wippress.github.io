WIP Press is a digital composing stick for MA Graphic Communication Design In Progress 2027. Type the keyword you chose with your partner. As in letterpress, the space bar does nothing here: every gap is built from lead spacers dragged in from the case. Print pulls a proof in the WIP Press Black Scan founts, giving each letter one of nine scanned impressions at random. Save the proof as a PNG with both your names.
